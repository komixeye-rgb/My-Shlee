Shlee Student Dashboard

Native Android App (Kotlin & Jetpack Compose)

The complete examination prep hub for Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) and Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) candidates across Uganda.

📊 Subject Mastery & Radar

Interactive radar charts mapping competence across UNEB subjects against the 80% Distinction 1 benchmark line.

🏆 Ugandan Leaderboard

Real-time national rankings for schools like King's College Budo, St. Mary's Kitende, Gayaza High, and Namagunga.

📱 Client-Side QR Sharing

Authentic scannable student revision badge with one-click link sharing and streak verification.

🎯 Daily Goals & Progress Ring

Circular progress indicators tracking daily study tasks, streaks, and custom notification alarms.

🧠 High-Thinking AI Tutor

Powered by gemini-4-argon with thinkingLevel = HIGH for deep step-by-step UNEB question breakdowns.

📱 How to Run or Install this App

  1. Live Emulator Preview: Use the interactive Android emulator streaming directly inside Google AI Studio.
  2. Export & Build APK/AAB: Download the source code zip or open directly in Android Studio to assemble the APK/AAB for distribution via Google Play Store or Firebase App Distribution.
